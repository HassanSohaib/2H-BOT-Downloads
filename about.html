About 2H BOT

A focused Windows tool for organized Marketplace work.

2H BOT is developed by the 2H BOT team for users who want to keep repeatable Facebook Marketplace listing information, campaign data and bot-managed profiles organized in one Windows desktop application. The goal is to reduce repeated data entry while keeping the workflow visible and user-controlled.

The software includes campaign data groups, draft and listing-management workflows, local profile handling and utility areas for different Marketplace tasks. Users are responsible for how they use the tool and for complying with the rules that apply to their accounts and listings.
